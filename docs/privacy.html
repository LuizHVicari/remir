Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Remir is a personal project by Luiz Vicari ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the Remir app and its API collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information I collect

  • Account information. When you sign up or sign in, authentication is handled by Clerk. Clerk receives your email address and, if you use Google sign-in, the basic profile information Google shares (name, email address and profile picture).
  • Information stored by the Remir API. My server stores your Clerk user identifier, your display name, and the dates your profile was created and updated.
  • Technical information. Like most services, the server may log technical details of requests, such as IP address, time and requested path, to keep the service running and secure.

How I use it

Only to provide the app: to sign you in, to show and update your profile, and to keep the service secure. I do not sell your information, I do not show ads, and I do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Service providers

  • Clerk (authentication and user accounts). See Clerk's privacy policy. Clerk may also collect limited technical diagnostics from its SDK.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloud hosting provider that runs the API and its database.

These providers process data only to provide their services. I do not share your information with anyone else, except when required by law.

Retention and deletion

I keep your information while your account exists. You can ask me to delete your account and the data stored about you by emailing the address below; I will act on it within a reasonable time.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Remir is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the effective date above.
