# Privacy Policy

Remir is a personal life-management app (expenses, church, habits, work, study and fitness). It is operated by Luiz Vicari ("I", "me"), who is the controller of the personal data described here. This policy explains what data the app and its API process, why, who else handles it, and what choices you have.

## Data I process

**Account data, handled by Clerk.** Sign-in and account management are provided by Clerk. When you create an account or sign in, Clerk processes your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile information Google shares (name, email address and profile picture). Clerk also processes authentication and session data, such as device and browser details, IP address and cookies needed to keep you signed in and to prevent fraud.

**Data stored by the Remir API.** My server stores your Clerk user identifier, your display name, the dates your profile was created and updated, and a record of which version of this policy and of the Terms of Service you accepted and when.

**The information you enter in the app.** As features are added, the data you enter (for example expenses, habits or notes) is stored on my server and linked to your account. It is yours, and I use it only to run the app for you.

**Technical data.** Like most services, my server and hosting providers may log technical details of requests (IP address, time, requested path, error information) to keep the service running and secure.

## Why I use it

To create your account and sign you in, to provide and improve the app's features, to keep the service secure and prevent abuse, to keep proof that you accepted these documents, and to comply with legal obligations. I do not sell your personal data, I do not show ads, and I do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

## Who else handles your data

I use these providers to run the service. They process data only to provide their services:

- **Clerk**, for authentication and user accounts. Clerk acts as a processor on my behalf. See [Clerk's privacy policy](https://clerk.com/legal/privacy).
- **Google**, only if you choose to sign in with Google. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Hostinger**, which hosts the server that runs the API.
- **Cloudflare**, which routes requests to the server and therefore sees them in transit (including IP addresses).
- **Neon**, which hosts the database.

If paid plans are introduced, payments will be handled by a third-party payment provider, and I will update this policy before that happens. I do not share your data with anyone else, except when required by law.

## Where your data is processed

My providers may store and process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, I rely on the safeguards offered by those providers for international transfers.

## How long I keep it

I keep your data while your account exists. When you ask me to delete your account, I delete the data stored on my server about you; copies in backups are removed as those backups are rotated out. Clerk deletes the account data it holds according to its own policy. I may keep the minimum records I am legally required to keep.

## Your rights

You can ask me to confirm that I process your data, to give you access to it, to correct it, to delete it, to export it, or to stop processing it, and you can withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise a right, write to the email address below. I will answer within the period required by law. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

## Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, and access to the server is restricted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your information.

## Children

Remir is not directed to children under 16, and I do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given me personal data, write to me and I will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

When I change this policy I publish a new version. If the change is material, the app asks you to review and accept it again before you continue.
